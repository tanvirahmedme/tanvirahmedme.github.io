My research was featured in **Hillviews Magazine**, Texas State University's official publication, in a profile titled *"Intelligent Agriculture: building a data-driven future for cattle care."* The article covers my work on the [AnimalCareBot](https://hillviews.txst.edu/articles/2026/intelligent-agriculture.html) project — a smart cattle collar with sensors and machine learning, integrated with an autonomous robot for real-time livestock monitoring.
